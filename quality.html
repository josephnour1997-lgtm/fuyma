Quality Assurance

Quality is Not Optional

From material traceability through to final dimensional verification – every part leaves FUYMA with documented quality evidence backed by internationally recognised certifications.

Inspection Methods

Three Pillars of Quality Control

We deploy advanced, complementary inspection techniques to verify material composition, internal integrity and dimensional accuracy on every production run.

SPCTR

Digital Spectrometer

Material composition is verified using a digital spectrometer that analyses the exact elemental make-up of every batch of metal. Ensures alloy compliance before a single shot is made – providing full material traceability from ingot to finished part.

X-RAY

X-Ray Inspection

Industrial X-ray equipment inspects finished castings for internal defects including porosity, inclusions and cold shuts – defects invisible to the naked eye. Essential for safety-critical, hydraulic or pneumatic applications.

CMM

3D Coordinate Measurement

Our Coordinate Measuring Machine provides three-dimensional verification of part geometry against nominal CAD data – detecting and controlling dimensional errors in both casting and machining operations.
