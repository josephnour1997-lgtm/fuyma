Why Choose FUYMA

Built on Precision & Innovation

Over three decades of continuous investment in technology and people -- delivering quality parts for demanding industries worldwide.

Aluminium Die Casting

High pressure injection of aluminium, zamak and brass alloys with machines ranging from 60 to 1,600 tonnes, producing parts up to 15 kg.

Induction Melting

Medium-frequency induction furnace (450 kW, 860 kg capacity, 960 kg/h output) -- cutting-edge technology that reduces porosity and raises part quality.

In-House Tooling

Moulds designed and manufactured internally, applying direct foundry experience to ensure optimal filling, solidification and part performance.

Engineering Support

Co-design service from concept to production -- working with IGES, STEP, PARASOLID and PRT formats, with fill simulation and finite element analysis.

Automated Cells

Fully automated production islands with high-level process control, enabling minimal porosity and meeting strict sealing requirements.

Technical Partnership

5-time John Deere “Partner” award winner, recognised for quality, technical support, delivery reliability, cost and component performance.
