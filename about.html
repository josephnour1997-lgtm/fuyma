Since 1987

A Legacy Built on Investment

FUYMA was founded by D. Feliciano Arias in 1987. The company's guiding philosophy has always been simple:

"To grow, one must never stop investing."

This ethos has driven every major expansion, from our first injection machine through to today's fully automated production cells.

1987
Foundation
D. Feliciano Arias founds FUYMA and begins die-casting operations in aluminium, zamak and brass.
~1990
First Injection Machine
The first 60-tonne injection machine is commissioned, formally launching foundry production operations.
2005
Barajas T4 Terminal
FUYMA-manufactured fittings and connectors are selected for Madrid's landmark Barajas T4 international airport terminal.
2008
First John Deere Partner Award
FUYMA receives its first John Deere "Partner" supplier award, recognising excellence in quality, technical support, delivery reliability and cost.
2011
Induction Furnace Investment
Investment in a medium-frequency induction furnace: 450 kW, 860 kg melt capacity, 960 kg/hour. FUYMA becomes a pioneer of induction melting in Spanish die casting.
2012
John Deere Hall of Fame
After five consecutive partner awards, FUYMA is inducted into the John Deere Hall of Fame at their global headquarters in Moline, USA.
2013
New Leak-Testing Facility
Investment in dedicated leak-testing infrastructure for high-sealing applications.
Today
Global Precision Manufacturer
Operating 60–1,600 tonne machines, producing parts up to 15 kg for global clients across automotive, agricultural, industrial and lighting sectors.

September 2025

FUYMA Receives Public Grant for Acquisition of Production Equipment

FUYMA has been awarded funding by the Direccion General de Economia e Industria of the Comunidad de Madrid under the industrial aid programme. The grant finances 39% of eligible capital expenditure for 2025.

September 2013

FUYMA Opens New Leak-Testing Facilities

FUYMA invested in dedicated leak-testing installations, expanding quality control capabilities for components with high sealing requirements.

November 2012

FUYMA Inducted into John Deere Hall of Fame

After five consecutive years as a John Deere "Partner" supplier, FUYMA was inducted into the Hall of Fame at John Deere's global headquarters in Moline, USA.

September 2011

FUYMA Exhibits at CUMBRE 2011 – Bilbao Exhibition Centre

FUYMA presented its products and capabilities at the CUMBRE 2011 trade fair at the Bilbao Exhibition Centre (BEC).

April 2011

Investment in Medium-Frequency Induction Furnace

FUYMA announced a major investment in induction melting technology: a 450 kW furnace with 860 kg melt capacity and 960 kg/hour output – making FUYMA a pioneer of induction melting for aluminium injection in Spain.

November 2008

FUYMA Wins Inaugural John Deere Partner Award

FUYMA was honoured with the John Deere "Partner" supplier award for the first time, recognised for outstanding quality, technical support, delivery reliability, cost and component performance.

January 2005

FUYMA Components Selected for Barajas T4 Terminal

FUYMA-manufactured fittings and connectors were used in Madrid Barajas Airport's Terminal 4 expansion – demonstrating FUYMA's capacity for large-scale infrastructure supply.
