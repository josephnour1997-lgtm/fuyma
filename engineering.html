Virtual Validation

Predict Defects Before Production

Our simulation suite analyses every aspect of the die casting process digitally – eliminating costly errors and reducing time to first good part. Scroll through each simulation type below.

01 — Filling

Fill Simulation

Models exactly how liquid aluminium travels through the runner system and fills the die cavity under high pressure. We identify potential problems before a single gram of metal is poured in production.

Tracks the metal front as it advances through the cavity, revealing flow patterns and turbulence zones
Detects premature solidification that causes cold shuts or misruns before they occur in tooling
Identifies air entrapment pockets that would otherwise create porosity or surface defects
Optimises gate position, runner geometry and injection speed for uniform, controlled filling
Reduces trial-and-error on the press, cutting tooling modification costs and lead time
02 — Layout

Layout & Cooling Simulation

Simulates the full thermal layout of the mould – cooling channels, runner system and die geometry – to achieve optimal thermal balance and the shortest possible cycle time.

Maps heat distribution across the entire mould to balance temperatures between cavities and slides
Positions and sizes cooling channels to extract heat evenly, avoiding hot zones that distort parts
Predicts solidification sequence to ensure directional cooling toward the gate
Minimises cycle time by confirming that cooling is complete before ejection – without overchilling
Eliminates warpage and dimensional variation caused by uneven thermal contraction
03 — Porosity

Porosity Risk Analysis

Identifies regions of the part susceptible to gas or shrinkage porosity before the mould is manufactured. Critical for components with sealing, pressure or structural requirements.

Predicts gas porosity from entrapped air and die release agent gases during injection
Maps shrinkage porosity risk in areas that solidify last and are isolated from the feed metal
Highlights critical zones in components requiring leak-tightness or X-ray acceptance
Informs overflow and vent placement to guide trapped gases safely out of the cavity
Combined with fill simulation, provides a full defect-risk picture that drives zero-defect tooling design
04 — Hot Zones

Hot Zone Analysis

Maps the thermal hot spots within the mould that accumulate excessive heat during the casting cycle. Uncontrolled hot zones accelerate die wear, cause soldering and increase scrap rates.

Identifies localised overheating in cores, slides and thin steel sections prone to heat build-up
Guides placement of targeted cooling inserts or baffles to extract heat precisely where needed
Predicts die soldering risk – where aluminium sticks to the steel surface due to excess temperature
Extends die service life by eliminating thermal fatigue cracking caused by repeated hot-cold cycling
Directly improves surface quality and dimensional consistency of cast parts across long production runs
